Why I Chose UMD

The Duluth Area

The first time I visited Duluth I was 10 years old and I immediately fell in love with Lake Supurior. When I came back to tour UMD in high school, my mom and I got chances to explore the area a little more and I found that Duluth area is beautiful. Not only is the area beautiful, but the people here are amazing too. I love Duluth!

Campus Size

When I was looking at colleges, I knew I wanted to go to a smaller school, but not too small. I wanted to be able to have a close community and be able to get to know new people as well. UMD is the perfect size for me. It's not too big where I would feel lost, but it's also not too small where I wouldn't have any opportunities. Also being indoors for the winters is very nice walking to classes instead of outside.

Dance Team

I have always been passionate about dance, and being able to join the UMD Dance Team was a major factor in my decision to attend. I wanted to continue my dancing career at a collegiate level and I am so grateful that UMDDT has given me that opportunity.The team is incredibly supportive and provides a great outlet for my creativity and love of performance.

What I Love About Duluth

Duluth has offered me many fun experiences and opportunities to explore the area. I have found so many fun things to do here while attending UMD. One thing we did a lot more this fall was bike around. We would bike to various sites around Duluth. We would find rivers and trails to go on. Another thing I love to do is to get ice cream and walk along the lake around sunset time. It's just unreal how beautiful the lake with the city look together.

Campus & City Highlights

Kirby Student Center

This is my favorite place to study on campus by far. There are also always various activites and things going on there.

The Lift Bridge

This is one of the most iconic landmarks in Duluth. The Aerial Lift Bridge is a historic suspension bridge that spans the Saint Louis River.

Amsoil Arena

Amsoil Arena is the home of UMD's hockey teams and is a great place to catch a game. The atmosphere is always electric, and it's a fantastic way to experience school spirit and support our teams.

Portlands Malt Shoppe

This is my favorite spot by the lake to get a sweet treat. Their malts are maybe the best malts I have ever had.
